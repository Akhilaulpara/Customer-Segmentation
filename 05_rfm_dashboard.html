Marketing playbook
SQL concepts showcased
NTILE(5) OVER (...)
Divides customers into 5 equal quantile buckets per dimension — no hard-coded thresholds, adapts to any data distribution.
Chained CTEs
Five sequential CTEs: cleaned → order_summary → rfm_raw → rfm_scored → rfm_segments. Each step independently auditable.
DATE_DIFF()
Recency anchored to MAX(date) + 1 day so scores are reproducible regardless of when the query runs.
SUM() OVER ()
Window aggregation for % of revenue computed inline across all segments — no self-join or correlated subquery needed.
COUNT(DISTINCT)
Frequency counts distinct invoices after collapsing line items — prevents multi-item baskets from inflating order counts.
CASE WHEN (ordered)
First-match ordered CASE logic ensures mutually exclusive segment assignment across all 6 business segments.
